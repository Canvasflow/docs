A publication is the top-level container for all of your content in Canvasflow. Before you can create articles, set up styles, or connect publish channels, you need at least one publication. Canvasflow supports two publication types — Article Based and Issue Based — each designed to match a different editorial workflow. Take a moment to understand the difference before you create your publication, because the type cannot be changed after creation.

## Publication Types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Article Based" icon="newspaper">
    Article Based publications support a **continuous publishing model**. Articles are managed and published individually, with no requirement to organise them into issues. This is the best choice for websites, blogs, newsletters, and any publication that releases content on an ongoing basis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Issue Based" icon="book">
    Issue Based publications support the **traditional print-style issue model**. Articles are grouped and managed within issues — ideal for magazines, journals, and any publication that releases content in discrete, dated editions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  A publication's type **cannot be changed** once it has been created. Choose carefully before clicking **Create**.
</Warning>

## Create a Publication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Brand Manager">
    Log in to Canvasflow and navigate to the **Brand Manager**. This is your central hub for all publications in your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the creation flow">
    Click **Add Publication**. The **Create Publication** window will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a publication type">
    Choose either **Article Based** or **Issue Based** depending on your editorial model. Refer to the descriptions above if you are unsure which to pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your publication">
    Enter a **Name** for your publication. Optionally, add a **Description** to help your team identify it. Both fields are for internal use only and can be updated at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the publication">
    Click **Create**. Your new publication will be created and will appear in the Brand Manager, ready for you to add articles, styles, and publish channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Publication names and descriptions are **internal only** — they are not visible to readers or included in published content. You can change both fields at any time from the publication settings.
</Note>

## Working with Issues (Issue Based Only)

If you created an Issue Based publication, you need to create at least one issue before you can organise articles within it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Issue Manager">
    Navigate to your publication and open the **Issue Manager**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new issue">
    Click **Create Issue**. The **Create Issue** window will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your issue">
    Give your issue a **Name** and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm creation">
    Click **Create**. The issue will appear in the Issue Manager, and you can begin adding articles to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
