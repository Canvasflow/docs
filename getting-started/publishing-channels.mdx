Publish channels are the integrations that connect your Canvasflow publication to the platforms where your readers consume content. Once a channel is connected, you can push articles directly from the Article Builder with a single action — no manual exporting, no copy-pasting, no developer involvement required. Canvasflow supports a broad range of channels, from web apps and social platforms to PDF generation and specialist distribution networks, so you can reach your audience wherever they are.

There is no limit to the number of channels you can connect to a single publication. Each channel you add becomes available as a publish target from within the Article Builder, and each article will display its publish state (for example, **Unpublished**, **Published**, or **Published but not up to date**) for every connected channel.

## Add a Publish Channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Publication Settings">
    Navigate to the publication you want to configure, then click **Settings** in the publication toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Publish Channels tab">
    Select the **Publish Channels** tab. Any channels already connected to the publication will be listed here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new channel">
    Click **Add Channel** to open the channel configuration panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the channel type">
    From the **Channel Type** menu, choose the platform you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your channel credentials">
    Provide the credentials or connection details required for the selected channel type. The exact fields will vary depending on the channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the channel">
    Click **Add Channel** to save the configuration. The new channel will appear in the Publish Channels list and will be immediately available as a publish target in your Article Builder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Publishing options in the Article Builder are driven by the channels you connect here. If you do not see a publish option for a particular platform, check that the corresponding channel has been added and configured in Publication Settings.
</Note>

## Supported Publish Channels

Canvasflow supports the following publish channels:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="PWA (Progressive Web App)" icon="globe">
    Publish your content as a fully hosted, responsive progressive web app. Readers can access your PWA through any browser, and it can be added to the home screen of any device for an app-like experience.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apple News" icon="apple">
    Distribute content directly to the Apple News platform, reaching iOS and macOS readers through the native News app. Canvasflow automatically formats your articles to meet Apple News requirements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WordPress" icon="wordpress">
    Push articles from Canvasflow to your WordPress site, or pull content from WordPress into Canvasflow. The bidirectional integration keeps both platforms in sync.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Twixl Publisher" icon="mobile">
    Publish articles as Twixl content items, collections, and full app publications. Canvasflow supports Twixl cell styles and all standard Twixl content models.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PDF" icon="file-pdf">
    Generate print-ready or on-demand PDFs from any article. Ideal for archived editions, downloadable reports, or print supplements.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HTML Email" icon="envelope">
    Convert your articles into distribution-ready HTML emails. Send polished, branded newsletters without needing a separate email design tool.
  </Card>
  <Card title="XML" icon="code">
    Export article content as structured XML for ingestion by downstream systems, print workflows, or third-party platforms that require a raw data feed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zinio" icon="tablet">
    Publish digital magazine editions to the Zinio digital newsstand, making your publication available to Zinio's subscriber base across all devices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cafeyn" icon="book-open">
    Distribute content to Cafeyn (formerly Relay), a digital press and magazine aggregator with a large European readership.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Paperlit" icon="layer-group">
    Publish to Paperlit, a platform for creating and distributing digital publications and in-app content experiences.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HPub" icon="file-zipper">
    Export content in HPub format for use with Baker Framework-compatible apps and custom publication containers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Not sure which channels to connect first? Start with the channel that matches your primary audience — for most web-first publishers that is **PWA**, while magazine publishers typically start with **Twixl** or **Zinio**.
</Tip>

## Manage Existing Channels

All connected channels are listed on the **Publish Channels** tab in Publication Settings. From there you can edit channel credentials, reorder channels, or remove a channel that is no longer needed. Changes to channel configuration take effect immediately for all future publish actions.

<Warning>
  Removing a publish channel from your publication does not unpublish content that has already been sent to that channel. To unpublish content, use the relevant platform's own tools.
</Warning>
