Canvasflow is a cloud-based digital publishing platform that empowers editorial teams to create beautiful, responsive HTML5 content and distribute it across multiple channels — from progressive web apps and Apple News to WordPress, Twixl, and beyond. Whether you publish continuously or in print-style issues, Canvasflow gives you a single authoring environment that handles the complexity of multi-channel distribution, so your team can focus on creating great content.

## Key Concepts

Before you dive in, it helps to understand the core building blocks of the Canvasflow platform.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Publications" icon="book-open">
    A **Publication** is the top-level container for your content. Each publication is either **Article Based** (continuous publishing) or **Issue Based** (print-style issue model). All articles, styles, templates, and publish channels belong to a single publication.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Articles" icon="file-lines">
    An **Article** is an individual piece of content built inside the Article Builder. You populate articles by dragging and dropping components — headlines, paragraphs, images, and more — onto a responsive canvas.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Styles" icon="palette">
    A **Style** controls the visual design of an article: background colour, typography, font sizes, padding, and more. Styles are independent of content, so you can swap or update a style at any time without touching the article text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Templates" icon="table-columns">
    A **Template** defines the structural layout of an article — the arrangement of columns, text components, and rich media placeholders. Because styles are separate from templates, your design can evolve independently of your content structure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Publish Channels

Once your content is ready, Canvasflow can push it to a wide range of channels. Supported channels include **PWA**, **Apple News**, **WordPress**, **Twixl Publisher**, **PDF**, **HTML Email**, **XML**, **Zinio**, **Cafeyn**, **Paperlit**, and **HPub**. You connect channels from your publication's Settings page and there is no limit to the number of channels per publication.

## How the Workflow Fits Together

The typical Canvasflow workflow follows four steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Publication">
    Set up your publication in the Brand Manager, choosing between Article Based or Issue Based to match your editorial model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design Your Style">
    Open the Style Manager to define the typography, colours, and layout that represent your brand. Create as many styles as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build Your Articles">
    Use the Article Builder to drag and drop content components onto the canvas. Attach a style and, optionally, a template to keep your layouts consistent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to Your Channels">
    Connect one or more publish channels in Publication Settings, then publish directly from the Article Builder with a single action.
  </Step>
</Steps>
