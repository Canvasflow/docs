The **Article Manager** is the hub of your publication — the place where every piece of content lives, and where you go to create, organise, and manage articles. Canvasflow's Article Builder gives you an intuitive drag-and-drop canvas for assembling responsive HTML5 content without writing a single line of code. Follow the steps below to create your first article and get comfortable with the interface.

## Create a New Article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Article Manager">
    Navigate to your publication and open the **Article Manager**. If you have not created any articles yet, you will see a **Create Your First Article** button in the centre of the screen. Otherwise, click the **+** icon at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the article details">
    Complete the fields in the **Create Article** window:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Name** | The internal title of your article. |
    | **Description** | An optional summary for internal reference. |
    | **Template** | Choose a saved template to pre-populate the article layout, or select **None** to start with a blank canvas. |
    | **Style** | Select the style that controls the visual design of this article. You can change the style at any time from the Article Builder. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the article">
    Click **Create**. The new article will appear in the Article Manager. When your publication is connected to a publish channel, each article will also display its publish state — for example, **Unpublished**, **Published**, or **Published but not up to date**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Articles are specific to the publication in which they are created. They cannot be moved directly between publications.
</Note>

## Add Content in the Article Builder

Once you have created an article, open it to enter the **Article Builder** — your main content authoring workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Article Builder">
    Click your newly created article in the Article Manager. You will be taken directly to the Article Builder canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the component panel">
    On the left side of the screen you will find the **component panel**. It contains all available content components, grouped by type:

    - **Text components** — Headline, Title, Subtitle, Paragraph, Blockquote
    - **Image components** — Image, Gallery
    - **Rich media components** — Video, Audio, Map, Table, and more
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a text component">
    Drag a text component — such as **Headline** or **Paragraph** — from the component panel and drop it onto the canvas. As you drag, a position indicator shows exactly where the component will land, which is especially useful when placing content between existing components. Click the component on the canvas to start typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an image component">
    Drag an **Image** component from the panel onto the canvas. Click the image component to open the **Image Properties** panel. From here you can:

    - Upload or change the image
    - Add a caption
    - Stretch the image to the full width of the canvas
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue building your article">
    Keep adding components to build out your article. Each component type has its own properties panel that appears when you click it — use these panels to fine-tune content, formatting, and layout options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Re-order, Resize, and Remove Components

Canvasflow makes it straightforward to reorganise your layout at any point during authoring.

- **Re-order** — Hover over a component to reveal the **inline component controls** to its left. Use the drag handle to move the component up or down the canvas.
- **Remove** — Use the inline component controls to delete a component from the canvas.
- **Resize and column layouts** — For more complex layouts, Canvasflow supports multiple columns. Resize components and arrange them in columns to create the structure you need.

<Tip>
  All content in Canvasflow is **100% responsive** — your articles automatically adapt to any screen size without extra effort on your part.
</Tip>

## Preview on Desktop and Mobile

Use the **Workspace Manager** menu in the toolbar to switch the canvas between different device views — Desktop is the default. Canvasflow automatically remembers your last selected workspace for each publication.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workspace options">
    Click **Workspace** in the Article Builder toolbar. The list of available workspace sizes will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a workspace">
    Click the workspace you want — for example, **Mobile** — to resize the canvas to that environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the menu">
    Click anywhere outside the workspace menu to close it and continue editing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Switching the workspace only changes your editing view. It does not affect how the article is rendered for readers — Canvasflow handles responsive layout automatically across all devices.
</Info>
