Canvasflow's xFlow service automates the conversion of print-ready PDF files into fully responsive digital articles. Once set up, you deliver PDF files to a dedicated FTP location and xFlow picks them up, processes them, and creates Canvasflow articles — extracting text, images, and layout data automatically. This page explains how to configure your FTP client, deliver files correctly, and prepare your PDFs for the best possible output.

<Note>
  The xFlow service must be activated for your account before you can use it. Contact [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:support@canvasflow.io) to request activation and receive your FTP credentials.
</Note>

---

## xFlow FTP Setup

Canvasflow uses Amazon S3-compatible storage as the FTP endpoint. To connect, you need three pieces of information provided by the Canvasflow support team:

- **Access Key ID** — authenticates your identity (e.g. `BKIA9SPMJHIEZF4WYOSO`)
- **Secret Access Key** — used together with the Access Key ID to sign requests
- **Remote Path** — the target folder in the Canvasflow storage bucket (e.g. `/canvasflow-remote-path`)

### Configuring Your FTP Client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new connection in your FTP client">
    Create a new connection or bookmark in your chosen FTP client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the protocol to Amazon S3">
    In the protocol or connection-type selector, choose **Amazon S3** (sometimes labelled **S3**). Do not use SFTP or standard FTP — they will not connect to the Canvasflow endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the connection details">
    Fill in the fields using the credentials provided by Canvasflow:

    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | Protocol | Amazon S3 |
    | Address / Server | `s3.amazonaws.com` |
    | Access Key ID | *(provided by Canvasflow)* |
    | Secret Access Key | *(provided by Canvasflow)* |
    | Remote Path | *(provided by Canvasflow — must begin with `/`)* |

    <Warning>
      Always include the leading `/` in the remote path. Omitting it will prevent the client from connecting to the correct folder.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and verify">
    Connect to the server. If credentials are correct, you will see your designated upload folder. You are now ready to deliver PDF files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your workflow requires programmatic access via API rather than an FTP client, Canvasflow supports this. Contact [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:support@canvasflow.io) for API access details.
</Note>

## Delivering Files via FTP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to the FTP endpoint">
    Open your configured FTP client and connect using your xFlow credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the PDF">
    Drop your PDF file into the remote upload folder. The upload begins immediately. Depending on your internet connection speed, this may take anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes for large files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing to begin">
    Once the upload is complete, xFlow detects the file automatically — usually within a few seconds. When processing begins, the file is moved out of the upload folder and into the processing queue. The PDF will disappear from the upload folder at this point; this is expected behaviour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the processed article in Canvasflow">
    After processing is complete, log in to your Canvasflow account to view the resulting article. Processing time varies depending on file size and current queue activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your FTP account has **write-only** access to the upload folder. You can upload files, but you cannot edit or delete them. If you upload a file in error, contact the Canvasflow team at [production@canvasflow.io](mailto:production@canvasflow.io) to have it removed.
</Warning>

## Preparing Your PDF for Best Results

xFlow can process almost any PDF, but following these guidelines will produce the highest-quality output.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="PDF structure">
    - Send content as a **single, multi-page PDF** rather than multiple individual files.
    - Every page must be an **individual page** — do not use spreads.
    - Make sure pages are in the correct reading order.
    - Set trim-boxes accurately so that only the relevant page content is shown, with **no bleed or overlap** from adjacent pages.
    - Do not apply security restrictions or compression to the PDF.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Text and fonts">
    - Fully embed all fonts in the PDF.
    - Do not flatten, rasterise, or convert text to outlines/paths — xFlow needs live text to extract content accurately.
    - If you use glyph substitution or stylistic fonts that visually alter characters (e.g. small-caps), note that xFlow extracts the underlying character, not the visual representation. Use uppercase characters in the source so that the extracted text is uppercase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Images">
    - Avoid flattening images or merging layers where possible — keeping images as separate objects allows xFlow to extract them individually.
    - Do not down-sample images. Aim for a maximum width or height of **2,400 px** for large images to ensure a crisp appearance on screen.
    - **300 DPI or higher** is preferred and will produce the best results.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Replica Mode for PWA

If you publish your Canvasflow articles to the Progressive Web App (PWA) channel, xFlow supports a **Replica Mode** that preserves the visual layout of each PDF page as a high-fidelity image replica, while still making the article text searchable and accessible. Contact [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:support@canvasflow.io) to discuss whether Replica Mode is appropriate for your workflow.

## Getting Started with xFlow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request xFlow activation">
    Contact [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:support@canvasflow.io) to request activation of the xFlow service for your Canvasflow account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your FTP credentials">
    The Canvasflow support team will provide your **Access Key ID**, **Secret Access Key**, and **Remote Path**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your FTP client">
    Follow the [xFlow FTP Setup](#xflow-ftp-setup) steps above to connect your FTP client to the Canvasflow S3 endpoint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver your first PDF">
    Upload a test PDF to confirm the end-to-end workflow is working correctly before moving to production.
  </Step>
</Steps>
