The Canvasflow for WordPress plugin gives your editorial team a fast, code-free way to push blog posts and pages from WordPress directly into a Canvasflow publication. Once connected, a **Canvasflow Publish Widget** appears inside the WordPress post editor, letting you publish individual articles to Canvasflow without leaving your existing workflow. A bulk **Posts Manager** and **Upload Manager** are also available for managing larger volumes of content.

<Note>
  Before you begin, [request a WordPress API key](https://canvasflow.io/apikey-request) from Canvasflow to enable WordPress publishing for your account.
</Note>

---

## How the Integration Works

Publishing content from WordPress to Canvasflow is controlled from two places:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="WordPress Plugin (Push)" icon="wordpress">
    The plugin installed in your WordPress dashboard controls how content is **pushed** from WordPress — which publication it connects to, default style settings, and which post types are eligible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Canvasflow Connector (Process)" icon="gear">
    Settings inside your Canvasflow account control how content is **processed** once it arrives — how WordPress markup is converted into Canvasflow components and what elements are included or excluded.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Installing the Plugin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install from the WordPress Plugin Directory">
    In your WordPress dashboard, go to **Plugins → Add New** and search for **Canvasflow**. Click **Install Now**, then **Activate**.

    Alternatively, [download the plugin from wordpress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/canvasflow/) and extract the folder to `/wp-content/plugins/canvasflow`, then activate it from the Plugins page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm activation">
    After activation, a **Canvasflow** item appears in your WordPress dashboard sidebar. If you experience trouble activating the plugin, refer to the plugin troubleshooting guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configuring the Plugin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Canvasflow settings">
    In your WordPress dashboard, navigate to **Canvasflow → Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your API key">
    Paste the API key provided by Canvasflow into the **API Key** field and save. Canvasflow validates the key and syncs your available publications automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a publication">
    Choose the Canvasflow publication you want to connect to from the **Publication** drop-down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a default style">
    Select the Canvasflow style to apply to published articles by default. You can override this setting on a per-article basis when you publish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure advanced options">
    Adjust the following options to suit your workflow:

    | Option | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **Include Title** | When enabled, the WordPress post title is included in the content sent to Canvasflow. Disable this if you manage the title separately inside Canvasflow. |
    | **Auto Publish** | When enabled, articles are automatically published to the downstream distribution platform (e.g. a connected Twixl app) as soon as they arrive in Canvasflow. |
    | **Custom Post Types** | Add any custom post types registered in your WordPress instance so they become eligible for publishing. |
    | **Merge Adjacent Paragraphs** | When enabled (default), consecutive paragraphs are combined into a single Canvasflow text component. When disabled, each paragraph becomes its own individual component. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save** to apply the configuration. Your WordPress instance is now connected to Canvasflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you publish to an issue-based Canvasflow publication or a Twixl Publisher channel that uses Collections, an additional **Issue** or **Collection** selector will appear in the plugin settings and the publish widget.
</Tip>

## Publishing an Article from WordPress

With the plugin configured, you can publish any post or page to Canvasflow directly from the WordPress editor.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the post or page">
    In your WordPress dashboard, open the post or page you want to publish to Canvasflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Canvasflow Publish Widget">
    Find the **Canvasflow** widget in the editor sidebar — it appears alongside the Metatags, Featured Image, and Category panels.

    <Note>
      If the widget is not visible, click **Screen Options** at the top-right of the editor and ensure the Canvasflow panel is checked.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a style (optional)">
    Choose a Canvasflow style from the widget's style selector, or leave it set to the default you configured in the plugin settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to Canvasflow">
    Click **Publish to Canvasflow**. The article is sent to your connected publication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the publish status">
    When the publish succeeds, the widget displays a **Success** message and the article status changes to **In Sync**. If an **Error** message appears, verify that your API key and publication settings are correct, then try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Content in Bulk

For larger publishing operations, the **Posts Manager** and **Upload Manager** give you greater control over multiple articles.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Posts Manager">
    The Posts Manager lists all content in your WordPress instance — posts, pages, and any custom post types you have added. Use it to select which items should be made eligible for bulk publishing.

    1. Navigate to **Canvasflow → Posts Manager**.
    2. Select the articles you want to make eligible for publishing.
    3. Click **Save** to confirm. The selected articles will now appear in the Upload Manager.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Upload Manager">
    The Upload Manager shows all articles selected from the Posts Manager, as well as articles previously published via the publish widget. Each article displays a colour-coded upload icon:

    - **Grey** — The article has never been published to Canvasflow.
    - **Green** — The article is published and in sync with Canvasflow.
    - **Orange** — The article has been published previously but its content has changed since the last upload. Click the upload icon to push the updated version.

    You can also set or change the **Issue**, **Collection**, or **Style** for each article directly from this view.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Canvasflow Publish Widget is not visible in the editor">
    Click **Screen Options** at the top-right corner of the post editor and check the box next to **Canvasflow** to make the widget visible.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An error appears when publishing">
    Verify that the API key entered in **Canvasflow → Settings** is correct and matches the key provided to you. Also confirm that the correct publication is selected. If the issue persists, contact [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:support@canvasflow.io).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Published paragraphs appear as separate components">
    Check that **Merge Adjacent Paragraphs** is enabled in the plugin settings. When this option is disabled, each paragraph is treated as an independent Canvasflow component.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
