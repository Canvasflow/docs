The Publication Details tab is your central place for reviewing and updating the key properties of a publication, from its name and description through to its connected channels and text direction. Several fields are editable at any time, while others — such as the publication type and creation date — are fixed once set. This section also covers appearance customisation, auto-save preferences, and account-level options.

## Publication Details Overview

Each publication exposes the following fields on its Details tab:

| Field | Description | Editable |
|---|---|:---:|
| **Name** | The display name of your publication. | ✔ |
| **Description** | An optional short description of the publication. | ✔ |
| **Thumbnail** | The image used to identify the publication in the Brand Manager. Not used externally. | ✔ |
| **Text Direction** | The default text direction (e.g. left-to-right or right-to-left) for all articles in this publication. | ✔ |
| **Type** | Whether the publication is Article-based or Issue-based. | — |
| **Created on** | The date the publication was created. | — |
| **Created by** | The name of the user who created the publication. | — |
| **Connected Channels** | The publishing channels currently connected to this publication. | — |

## Editing Publication Name and Description

You can update the publication name and description at any time without affecting your content or connected channels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Publication Settings">
    From the **Brand Manager**, hover over the publication you want to edit and click the **Settings** (cog) icon. Alternatively, open the publication and click **Settings** in the admin toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Details tab">
    Select the **Details** tab from the Publication Settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter edit mode">
    Click the **Edit** button on the right side of the page and select **Edit** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the fields">
    The editable fields will become active. Update the **Name**, **Description**, **Thumbnail**, or **Text Direction** as required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The **Type** field (Article or Issue) cannot be changed after a publication has been created. If you need a different publication type, create a new publication.
</Note>

## Customising Publication Colours

Canvasflow lets you define a custom colour palette for each publication. These colours appear in the text formatting menu of the Article Editor, making it easy for your team to apply on-brand colours to inline text without needing to remember HEX values.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Publication Settings">
    From the **Brand Manager**, hover over the publication you want to customise and click the **Settings** (cog) icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Colours tab">
    Select the **Colours** tab from the Publication Settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a colour swatch">
    Click one of the colour swatches in the palette grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a HEX value">
    In the **HEX Colour** field beneath the swatch, type or paste the six-character HEX code for the colour you want to add (for example, `#EC5B6C`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the colour">
    Click **Update** to save the colour to the palette.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your updated colour palette is immediately available to all editors in the text formatting menu of the Article Editor.

<Tip>
  To find the HEX value of a colour, open any design application such as Adobe Photoshop or InDesign and use the colour picker tool. You can also use the free [HTML Color Codes Colour Picker](https://htmlcolorcodes.com/color-picker/) online.
</Tip>

## Auto-Save Settings

Canvasflow includes an optional auto-save feature that automatically saves your work in the Article Editor, Style Manager, and Template Manager at 15-second intervals. Auto-save is configured per user through **Profile Preferences** rather than at the publication level.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    From the **Brand Manager**, click your name in the menu bar to open your **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Preferences">
    Select **Preferences** from the left-hand panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Auto-Save">
    Check or uncheck the **Autosave** checkbox to enable or disable the feature.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Auto-save applies to article content, styles, and templates. Each individual user controls their own auto-save preference — enabling it for one team member does not affect others.
</Note>

## Forcing a Browser Refresh (Troubleshooting)

Modern browsers cache JavaScript and CSS resources to improve performance. Occasionally, after a Canvasflow platform update, your browser may continue to load an older cached version of the app. If you notice unexpected behaviour or missing features, a hard refresh will force your browser to load the latest version of the platform.

<Accordion title="How to perform a hard refresh">

**Google Chrome — Windows / Linux:**
- Hold **Ctrl** and click the **Reload** button, or press **Ctrl + F5**.
- For a full cache clear: press **F12** to open DevTools, then right-click the **Reload** button and choose **Empty Cache and Hard Reload**.

**Google Chrome — Mac:**
- Hold **⇧ Shift** and click the **Reload** button, or press **⌘ Cmd + ⇧ Shift + R**.

**Mozilla Firefox — Windows / Linux:**
- Press **Ctrl + F5**, or hold **Ctrl + ⇧ Shift** and press **R**.

**Mozilla Firefox — Mac:**
- Hold **⇧ Shift** and click the **Reload** button, or press **⌘ Cmd + ⇧ Shift + R**.

**Microsoft Edge / Internet Explorer:**
- Press **Ctrl + F5**, or hold **Ctrl** and click the **Refresh** button.

</Accordion>

<Tip>
  If a hard refresh does not resolve the issue, try clearing your browser's full cache and cookies, then reloading the Canvasflow app.
</Tip>

## Cancelling Your Account

If you need to close your Canvasflow account, contact the support team directly — account cancellation is handled manually to ensure all your data is managed correctly.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and cannot be reversed**. All publications, articles, assets, and settings associated with your account will be permanently removed. Make sure you have exported or backed up any content you wish to keep before submitting a cancellation request.
</Warning>

To cancel your account, send an email to [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:support@canvasflow.io) with the subject line **Cancel Account**. The support team will confirm the cancellation and guide you through the process.
