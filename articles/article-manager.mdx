The Article Manager is the central hub of every Canvasflow publication. From here you create new articles, find existing ones, edit their settings, control their order, and manage metadata — all before ever opening the editor. Whether you are working in an issue-based or article-based publication, the Article Manager is where every piece of content begins.

## Creating an Article

Every article starts in the Article Manager. You can create a blank article or base one on an existing template.

### Blank article

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your publication">
    From the dashboard, select the publication you want to work on. If your publication uses issues, select the issue you want to create the article in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new article">
    In the Article Manager, click the **Add Article** button at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe the article">
    Give your article a name and, optionally, a description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose No Template">
    Select **No Template** to start with a blank canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a style">
    Choose the style you want to apply to the article.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. Your new article appears in the Article Manager list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Article from a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add Article">
    In the Article Manager, click the **Add Article** button at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe the article">
    Enter a name and description for the new article.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    From the Template List, select the template you want to use as the starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create**. The article is created with all the components and layout from the template, using the style associated with that template.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When creating an article from a template, the style selector is not available — the article inherits the style tied to the chosen template. You can change the style at any time from within the Article Manager.
</Note>

## Searching for an Article

When your publication contains many articles, use the search feature to find what you need instantly. Results update as you type and become more precise with each additional character. The total number of matching results appears next to the search box.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Article Manager">
    Navigate to the Article Manager and ensure you are in the **Default** channel view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type in the search box">
    Begin typing the article name or author into the search box. Matching articles appear immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset the search">
    To show all articles again, clear the text from the search box.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can search by either the **Title** or **Author** column.
</Info>

## Editing Article Metadata

Metadata describes specific aspects of an article — such as creation date, modified date, and social sharing text. Canvasflow shows only channel-specific metadata, so you only see fields relevant to your connected publishing platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the article">
    In the Article Manager, find the article whose metadata you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Metadata panel">
    From the edit toolbar, click the **Metadata** button. The metadata panel appears on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or update fields">
    Fill in the required metadata fields. For Twixl, changes sync automatically with the distribution platform — no republishing is needed. For Pugpig and Purple DS channels, select **Edit** from the article toolbar to access fields such as Description and Category.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply changes">
    Click **OK** to save the metadata.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Metadata options are not displayed if your publication is not connected to a publishing channel.
</Note>

## Editing an Article's Name and Style

You can rename an article, update its description, or swap its associated style at any time from the Article Manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open article settings">
    In the Article Manager, locate the article and click the **cog** button in the Options column. The Edit Article window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Edit the name, description, or select a different style from the style dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update** to apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ordering Articles

When your publication is connected to a channel that supports ordering, you can drag articles into any sequence you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Article Manager">
    Navigate to the Article Manager and switch to the relevant channel view (ordering is not available in the Default view).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover over the article">
    Hover over the article you want to reorder to reveal the reorder handle on the far left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag to the new position">
    Click and hold the reorder handle, then drag the article up or down to the desired position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release">
    Release the handle to place the article in its new position.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Ordering is only available in a channel view, not the Default view. Unpublished articles cannot be reordered and will appear at the bottom of the list.
</Warning>

## Duplicating an Article

Duplicating an article creates an exact copy, which is useful for recurring content formats such as a monthly feature.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open article settings">
    In the Article Manager, click the **cog** button on the article you want to duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Duplicate">
    Click **Duplicate** from the menu. A confirmation pop-up appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**. The article is duplicated with an amended title appended to the original name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Duplicating articles is a great way to create reusable starting points for content types you produce frequently, such as interview features or product spotlights.
</Tip>

## Deleting an Article

If an article is no longer needed, you can permanently remove it from the Article Manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the article">
    In the Article Manager, find the article you want to delete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Trash icon">
    In the Options column, click the **Trash** icon. A confirmation prompt appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Click **Delete**. The article is removed from the Article Manager.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting an article is permanent and cannot be reversed. Make sure you no longer need the content before proceeding.
</Warning>

## Channel Views

Once a publishing channel has been configured for your publication, you can switch to that channel's view using the **Channel View** menu in the Article Manager.

Selecting a channel view shows contextual information for that platform — for example, when viewing the Twixl Publisher channel you can see which articles are published, their sync state, and access Twixl-specific metadata options. If your connected channel supports external content types, those articles also appear in the list.

Selecting **Default** shows all articles created in Canvasflow. It does not include any externally generated content.

<Info>
  Use the Default channel view for general article management such as searching, creating, and editing. Switch to a specific channel view when you need to manage publishing status, ordering, or channel metadata.
</Info>

## Text-to-Speech

Canvasflow can automatically generate clear audio files from the text content of your articles, making it easy to offer a listening experience for your readers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Brand Manager">
    Go to the **Brand Manager** and select the **Settings** icon for your publication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Publish Channels">
    Select **Publish Channels**, then click **Edit** next to the PWA channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable TTS">
    Ensure that **TTS** is enabled in the PWA Channel Configuration page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your issue">
    Publish the issue or issues you want to generate TTS audio for. Audio files are generated automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Text-to-Speech is an additional paid service. Contact the Canvasflow team for pricing and activation details.

  If multi-language is enabled on your publication, Canvasflow generates TTS audio files for all enabled languages. The TTS playback option is only shown when the PWA is in Responsive Mode — it is hidden in Replica Mode.
</Note>
