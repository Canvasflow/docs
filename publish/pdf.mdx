Canvasflow lets you generate a PDF from any article in just a few clicks, directly from the Article Manager. The conversion happens on the fly, so no additional tools or export configuration are required — your download begins as soon as you confirm the request.

## Creating a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Article Manager">
    Navigate to the **Article Manager** of the brand that contains the article you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the article's Edit menu">
    Locate the article you want to download and click the **spanner icon** to open its `Edit` menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Download › PDF">
    Choose `Download`, then select `PDF` from the flyout menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the timeout option">
    A confirmation popup will appear. For most articles the default timeout setting is sufficient. If your article is particularly long or contains complex content, change the **Timeout** dropdown to `Extended timeout` to allow more time for the conversion to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and download">
    Click `Confirm`. Your PDF download will begin immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Extended Timeout for Long Articles

PDF conversion in Canvasflow takes place on the fly — the system renders your article content into a PDF document at the moment you request it. For short or straightforward articles this process completes quickly, but longer articles with many images, complex layouts, or a high page count may require additional processing time.

<Note>
  If your download does not complete and you see a timeout error, repeat the steps above and select `Extended timeout` from the Timeout dropdown before clicking `Confirm`. You do not need to make any changes to your article content before retrying.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you regularly export lengthy articles, selecting **Extended timeout** as a precaution will not slow down the export for shorter content — it simply allows the process more time to finish before the connection is closed.
</Tip>

## On-the-Fly Conversion

Unlike some publishing channels that require a separate render step, the PDF export in Canvasflow is generated in real time each time you request it. This means the PDF always reflects the current saved state of the article at the moment of download — there is no separate publish or sync step required before exporting.

<Info>
  The PDF export is independent of your publish channels. You can download a PDF from any article regardless of whether it has been published to a distribution platform.
</Info>
