Canvasflow can transform any article into a fully responsive HTML email, ready to paste directly into your preferred email distribution platform. This lets you repurpose content created in the Canvasflow editor for email campaigns without any additional design work or coding.

## Generating an HTML Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Article Manager">
    Select the brand you require and open the **Article Manager**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the article settings">
    Click the **Settings icon** (cog) on the article you want to convert to an HTML email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Download">
    Choose `Download` from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select HTML Email">
    From the flyout, select `HTML Email` and confirm your choice in the popup that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the HTML document">
    A browser window or tab will open, displaying the article's content rendered as an HTML document. This is the fully responsive email output.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Extracting the HTML from Your Browser

Once the HTML email has opened in your browser, you need to copy the raw HTML source code to paste it into your email distribution platform. The method varies slightly depending on which browser you are using.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Chrome and Firefox">
    1. Right-click anywhere on the page and select **View Page Source** (Chrome) or **Page Source** (Firefox). A new tab or window will open showing the raw HTML.
    2. Press `Ctrl+A` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+A` (Mac) to select all the HTML content.
    3. Press `Ctrl+C` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+C` (Mac) to copy it to your clipboard.
    4. Switch to your email distribution platform and paste the HTML using `Ctrl+V` or `Cmd+V`, or via the right-click paste option.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Safari">
    1. Use the keyboard shortcut `Option+Command+U` to open the page source view.
    2. Press `Ctrl+A` or `Cmd+A` to highlight the entire page source.
    3. Press `Ctrl+C` or `Cmd+C` to copy the contents to your clipboard.
    4. Paste the HTML into your email distribution platform using `Ctrl+V` or `Cmd+V`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Always copy from the **page source view**, not by selecting text on the rendered page itself. Copying rendered text will not include the HTML markup needed by your email platform.
</Note>

## Testing Your HTML Email

Before sending your email to subscribers, it is good practice to test how it renders across different email clients and devices.

<Tip>
  Use [Putsmail](https://putsmail.com/tests/new) to send a test version of your HTML email to one or more addresses before deploying it through your distribution platform. Putsmail lets you paste your HTML directly and send a real test message, helping you catch any rendering issues early.
</Tip>

When testing, check the following:

- **Responsive layout** — confirm the email adapts correctly on both desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- **Images** — verify all images load and are not blocked by your email client.
- **Links** — click every link to ensure they resolve to the correct destinations.
- **Text formatting** — confirm fonts, sizes, and colours appear as expected across different email clients such as Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail.
