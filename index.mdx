## How Canvasflow Works

Canvasflow organises your content around **Publications** — a publication is the container for all your articles, styles, templates, and publish channels. Within a publication you create **Articles** using the drag-and-drop Article Builder, apply **Styles** to control design and typography, and use **Templates** to accelerate new article creation.

When your content is ready, you publish it to one or more connected channels with a single click.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a publication">
    Set up an article-based or issue-based publication from the Brand Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design your style">
    Build a style that defines your brand's typography, colours, and layout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Author your articles">
    Use the Article Builder to drag and drop text, images, video, and rich media components onto the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish everywhere">
    Connect a publish channel (PWA, Apple News, WordPress, and more) and publish with one click.
  </Step>
</Steps>
