Publications are the top-level containers for all of your content in Canvasflow. Every article, issue, style, and template you create lives inside a publication, and all publications are organised from the **Brand Manager** — the first screen you see when you sign in to your account. You can create as many publications as you need, connect each one to one or more publishing channels, and share users across them, though all other assets remain specific to the publication in which they were created.

## Brand Manager Overview

The Brand Manager is your central hub for managing all publications within your Canvasflow account.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Publications as containers" icon="box">
    Each publication holds all related articles, issues, styles, and templates. Assets created inside a publication are only accessible within that publication.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shared users" icon="users">
    Users are the only resource that can be shared across publications. All other assets — articles, styles, templates, and issues — are publication-specific.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No publication limit" icon="infinity">
    There is no limit to the number of publications you can create within your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multiple publish channels" icon="share-nodes">
    Each publication can be connected to one or more publishing channels, allowing you to deliver content to multiple destinations.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Creating a Publication

To add a new publication, follow the steps below from within the Brand Manager.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create Publication window">
    Navigate to the **Brand Manager** and click **Add Publication**. The **Create Publication** window will appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a publication type">
    Select the publication type that matches your content model. See the [Publication Types](#publication-types) section below for guidance on which to choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your publication">
    Enter a name for your publication. Optionally, add a description to help identify it within the Brand Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the publication">
    Click **Create**. Your new publication will be created and will appear in the Brand Manager.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The publication name and description are for internal use only and can be changed at any time. However, the publication **type cannot be changed** once it has been created — choose carefully.
</Note>

### Publication Types

Canvasflow supports two publication types. Select the one that best reflects how you intend to organise and publish your content.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Article Based" icon="file-lines">
    Supports a continuous publishing model. Articles are managed and published individually, without needing to be organised into issues. Ideal for news sites, blogs, and regularly updated digital publications.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Issue Based" icon="book-open">
    Supports a traditional print-style publishing model. Articles are organised and managed within discrete issues (for example, monthly editions). Ideal for magazines and periodicals.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  The publication type **cannot be changed** after the publication has been created. If you need a different type, you must create a new publication.
</Warning>

## Editing Publication Settings

You can update a publication's name, description, and other editable fields at any time through its settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the publication">
    From the **Brand Manager**, find the publication you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon (cog) on the publication card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Details tab">
    From the Settings area, click the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable editing">
    Click the **More Options** menu and select **Edit**. The editable fields will become active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Make the required changes and click **Save**. The publication details will be updated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For a full reference of all fields available on the Details tab — including thumbnail, text direction, and connected channels — see the [Publication Settings](/publications/publication-settings) page.
</Tip>

## Duplicating a Publication

Duplicating a publication (brand) is a quick way to reuse an existing setup — including its styles and configuration — as the starting point for a new publication.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the publication settings">
    From the **Brand Manager**, hover over the publication you want to duplicate and click the **Settings** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the Duplicate option">
    From the **Details** tab, click the **hamburger menu** (More Options) to reveal the **Duplicate** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm duplication">
    Select **Duplicate**. A confirmation box will appear — click **Confirm**. The duplicated publication will appear in the Brand Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rename the duplicate">
    Open the settings of the newly duplicated publication and update its name to distinguish it from the original.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If the original publication was connected to a publishing channel, you should remove or update the connector credentials on the duplicate. Having two publications synced to the same channel can cause unexpected publishing conflicts.
</Warning>

## Deleting a Publication

You can delete a publication at any time from within its settings.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the publication settings">
    From the **Brand Manager**, hover over the publication you want to delete and click the **Settings** icon (cog).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Details tab">
    From the Settings area, click the **Details** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete">
    Click the **More Options** menu and select **Delete**. A confirmation prompt will be displayed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    Click **Delete** to permanently remove the publication.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a publication is **permanent and cannot be reversed**. All articles, styles, and templates within the publication will be deleted at the same time. Ensure you have exported or backed up any content you wish to keep before proceeding.
</Warning>
