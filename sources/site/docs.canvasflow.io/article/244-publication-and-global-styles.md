# Brand and Global Styles

When creating or editing the properties of a style, there are two distinct options available. 'Brand', and 'Global'.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/603fc05c0a2dae5b58fb5887/file-TBqDu72ukV.png)

## Brand Style

A 'Brand Style' is only accessible by articles within the brand. It is the default style type and most commonly used in standard production.

## Global Style

A 'Global Style' is one that's available to every brand in your account.

This is useful for brands which share familiar components, for example those with several language translations.
