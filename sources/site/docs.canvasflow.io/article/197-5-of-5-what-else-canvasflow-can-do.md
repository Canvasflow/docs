# 5 of 5 - What else Canvasflow can do

You've covered building articles, styles and templates, but Canvasflow is more than just content authoring, it's an ecosystem designed to make publishing faster and simpler.

### With Canvasflow you can:

- Smart Sync with leading publishing platforms
- [Export from WordPress](https://wordpress.org/plugins/canvasflow/)
- [Publish to Wordpress, Medium, Apple News and more](https://blog.canvasflow.io/multi-channel-publishing/)
- Automate content production from external XML source
- Generate [on-demand PDF's](https://docs.canvasflow.io/article/182-creating-a-pdf-from-an-article)
- Build distribution ready HTML emails
