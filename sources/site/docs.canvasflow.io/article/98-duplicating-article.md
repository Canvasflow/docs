# Duplicating an Article

To duplicate an article:

1. Navigate to the Article Manager.
2. Each article has its own article settings menu which can be accessed clicking the spanner button.

 [![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab3bd332c7d3a56d88737d0/file-QHaRSUEhnB.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab3bd332c7d3a56d88737d0/file-QHaRSUEhnB.png)

3. Select '_Duplicate_', a pop up will display asking to confirm the duplication

 [![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab3c9422c7d3a56d88738b3/file-2VR5tbL6fI.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab3c9422c7d3a56d88738b3/file-2VR5tbL6fI.png)

4. Click 'Confirm', the article will be duplicated with an amended title.

### Note

- Duplicating content can be really useful for creating templates or example content pieces for those that are created often, such as a monthly feature pieces.
