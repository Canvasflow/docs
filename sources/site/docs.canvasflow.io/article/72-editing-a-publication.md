# Editing Publication Settings

1. From the 'Brand Manager', locate the publication you wish to edit
2. Click the `'settings'` icon.

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a8ef12704286305fbc9c290/file-PKlLg2OMUU.png)

3. From the 'Settings' area, click the `Details` tab.
4. Click the `More Options` menu and select `Edit`. The publication fields that can be changed will become editable.

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5e7cd3772c7d3a7e9ae9d80c/file-CUXGakHkgm.png)

5. Make the required changes and click `Save` to apply. The publication details will be updated.
