# Deleting an Article

If an article is no longer required, it can be deleted from the 'Article Manager'.

### To delete an article

1. Navigate to the 'Article Manager' and locate the article to be deleted. 

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a9435e82c7d3a54cdfcce61/file-Dz6zydyA9U.png)

2. From the `Options` column, click the `Trash` icon.
3. A confirmation prompt will be displayed.
4. Click `Delete`.  The article will be deleted.

### Notes:

1. Deleting an article is **permanent** and **cannot** be reversed**.**
