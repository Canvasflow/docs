# Managing the Table of Contents

By default, Canvasflow automatically generates and populates a 'Table of Contents' (ToC) for all published articles. You can also, if required, omit individual articles from the 'ToC'.

1. Navigate to 'Article Manager'.
2. Ensure the 'Channel View' is set to `PWA` .
3. Click the `Settings` button next to the desired article and select `Edit`.
4. Set the 'ToC Status' to `Excluded` .  This will prevent the article from being shown in the ToC. 

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/603f71f6b3ebfb109b587fb6/file-p5VcrnaaGS.png)

5. Previously omitted articles can be reinstated by changing the 'ToC Status' to `Include`. 
6. Click `Update` to confirm the change. 
7. [Republish](https://docs.canvasflow.io/article/273-publishing-content-to-a-pwa) the issue.
