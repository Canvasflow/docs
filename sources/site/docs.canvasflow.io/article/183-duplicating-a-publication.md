# Duplicating a Brand

Duplicating a brand is a quick and simple way to reuse existing content or styles as the basis for a new brand. 

**To duplicate a brand**

1. From 'Brand Manager', hover over your chosen brand and click the `Settings` icon.

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5c7d124a2c7d3a0cb9322e30/file-uP0E8eqm7z.png)

1. From `Details` , select the 'Hamburger Menu' to reveal `Duplicate` .

1. [![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5c7d19542c7d3a0cb9322e76/file-7Gz6p07rll.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5c7d19542c7d3a0cb9322e76/file-7Gz6p07rll.png)

2. The duplicate brand will appear in 'Brand Manager'.
3. Edit the new brand via settings and change the name.
4. From 'Brand Manager', hover over your chosen publication and click `Settings` .
5. From 'Details', select the hamburger menu to reveal the 'Duplicate' option.
6. Select `Duplicate` , a confirmation box will appear, click `Confirm` .

[![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5c7d195e04286350d0889739/file-uctwvjpS1f.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5c7d195e04286350d0889739/file-uctwvjpS1f.png)

### Notes

1. If the duplicated brand was connected to a publishing channel, we strongly recommend removing or updating the connector credentials to avoid issues that may occur when attempting to sync multiple publications to the same channel.
