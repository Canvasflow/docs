# Brand Manager Overview

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a954ef32c7d3a754951232a/file-E42pyYOUJb.png)

A publication acts as a container for your article content.  It is the top level of your account and consists of articles, issues, styles and templates.

All publications are organised from within the 'Brand Manager' and this is always the first screen you will see when signing into your account.

All Canvasflow assets are specific to a publication.  All articles, issues, styles and templates that are created are **only** accessible within the publication in which they were created.  The only resource that can be shared between publications are **users**.

There is no limit to the number of publications that can be created and each publication can be connected to one or more publishing channels.
