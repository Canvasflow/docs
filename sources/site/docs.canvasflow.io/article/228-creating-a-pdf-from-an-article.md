# Creating a PDF from an article

Canvasflow provides the ability to easily create a PDF from any article.

To create a PDF:

1. Navigate to the 'Article Manager' of the required brand.
2. Next to the article you want to download as a PDF, enter the `Edit` menu using the spanner icon.
3. Select `Download` , then `PDF` .

 [![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5cb5c6f80428631d263c2e0a/file-6nct8dg4mR.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5cb5c6f80428631d263c2e0a/file-6nct8dg4mR.png)

4. A confirmation popup will appear. As conversion to PDF format takes place on the fly, if the content is particularly long and complex, you may experience a timeout before the process completes. If necessary you can change the 'Timeout' dropdown option to `Extended timeout` , but for the majority of cases this isn't required.
5. Click `Confirm` . Your download will begin.
