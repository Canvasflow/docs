# Change the Article Style

Canvasflow supports the ability to dynamically change the style applied to an article.

#### Changing the style of an article:

1. From within the article builder, click the `Style` button on the article menu.  A list of all available styles will be displayed.

 [![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab40b782c7d3a56d8873c68/file-HsFu3OZinw.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab40b782c7d3a56d8873c68/file-HsFu3OZinw.png)

2. Click the style you want to apply to the article. The selected style will then be applied.
3. Click outside of the style menu to close it.
4. Click `Save` to confirm the style change.

#### Editing the currently selected style:

1. Save the article. Be aware that editing a current style will navigate you away from the current article.
2. Click the `Style` button from the article menu.  The current style will be selected.
3. Click `Edit` .

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5ab40bea2c7d3a56d8873c72/file-smxNUdyXr2.png)

4. You will be switched to the 'Style Builder'. The current style will be selected.
5. Make the required changes and click `Save` to update the style.
6. You can return to the article by clicking the 'Return to article' button.
