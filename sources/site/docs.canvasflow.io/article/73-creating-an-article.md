# Creating an Article

### Create a new article

1. From the dashboard, select the publication you wish to work on.
2. Select the issue you wish to create the article in.  _(If you are working in an article based publication, ignore this step)._
3. From the 'Article Manager', click the `Add Article` button at the top right. 

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a944341042863534055958a/file-4oFqDQqOLu.png)

4. Give your article a name and (optionally) a description.
5. To create a blank article, select `No Template`.
6. Select the style you wish to apply to the article.
7. Click 'Create'.  Your new article will be created and appear in the 'Article Manager' list.

### Creating an article from a template

1. From the 'Article Manager', click the `Add Article` button at the top right.
2. Give your new article a name and a description.
3. From the 'Template List', select the template you want to create the article from.

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a903bea2c7d3a54cdfcbdac/file-GQ4GJBn7jy.png)

4. When creating an article from a template, the option to choose a style is not available. The article will use the style associated with the chosen template.  The style can be changed at any time from within the 'Article Manager'.
5. Click `Create`.  Your new article will be created from the selected template and will appear in the article manager list.
