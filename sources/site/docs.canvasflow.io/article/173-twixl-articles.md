# Twixl Articles

When connected to a Twixl Publisher channel, additional options become available when creating and editing articles. These options are:

- **Content Type:** As Canvasflow Twixl integration supports Twixl content items, specific content items can be chosen, including PDF, MP4, Image, etc.
- **Cell Style:** This option allows a cell style (created within Twixl Distribution Platform) to be applied to a piece of content. See [more information on Twixl Cell Styles](https://help.twixlmedia.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000718205-Working-with-cell-styles).
 - Cell styles will be displayed with the name, and the column/row structure of that style. For example, "T: 6x3" indicates the cell style's tablet structure is 6 columns wide and 3 rows deep. "P: 4x2" would denote a cell style's phone structure is 4 columns wide, two deep.

### Article Manager options

In addition to the default article options, when connected to a Twixl channel, several other article controls also become available. These controls are:

- **Metadata** \- Edit the Author, Tagline, Category and Social Text.
- **Duplicate** - Duplicate an article or content item.
- **Move** \- Move an article or content item into a collection or different issue.
