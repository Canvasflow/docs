# Publishing to Cafeyn

### To publish to Cafeyn:

1. Ensure your issue has a ‘Release Date’ entered in ‘Issue Settings' by navigating to 'Issue Manager', and selecting the `settings` icon. 

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/603f80c18502d1120e90d25f/file-E5ZKXvv9cg.png)

2. Specify a release date (mandatory for Cafeyn publishing). 
3. Select 'Update'.
4. Navigate to ‘Article Manager’ view.
5. Publish all the articles intended for the Cafeyn issue by ticking the required checkboxes and choosing `Publish Selected` in the toolbar.

 ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/603f821624d2d21e45edc175/file-oDVXSN2U2B.png)

6. Once the articles are published, click the `Create Cafeyn` button in the toolbar. 
7. Once complete, you’ll have a Zip file downloaded which comprises of all associated images and a .json file, ready for upload.
8. Submit the files to Cafeyn via your usual method.
