# Deleting a Publication

1. From the Brand Manager, locate the publication you want to delete, click the `More Options` menu then `Settings`.
2. From 'Settings', click the `details` tab.
3. Click the `More Options` menu and select `Delete`.  A confirmation prompt will be displayed.
4. Click `Delete`.  The publication will be deleted.

### Notes:

- Deleting a publication is **permanent** and **cannot** be reversed.
- Deleting a publication will delete all **articles**, **styles** and **templates** within it**.**
