# Ordering Articles

When connected to a publishing channel that supports ordering, Canvasflow provides the ability to manage the order of articles.

![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a9027752c7d3a54cdfcbc8f/file-FdWmhDmNPb.png)

### To reorder an article

1. Open the 'Article Manager'.
2. Locate and hover over the article you wish to reorder.
3. On the far left, click and hold the `reorder handle`.
4. Drag the article up or down to set the new location.
5. Release the button to place the article in its new position.

### Notes:

- The ordering feature is subject to support by the connected publishing platform.
- Ordering is **not** supported in the 'Default' channel view.
- Unpublished articles **cannot** be reordered and will appear at the bottom of the 'Article Manager'.
