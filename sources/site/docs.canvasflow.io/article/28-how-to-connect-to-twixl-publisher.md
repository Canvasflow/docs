# How to connect to Twixl Publisher

Twixl Publisher is a cloud based publishing solution, designed to create and publish mobile content applications. In conjunction with Canvasflow you can create and enrich content, present content in an app, build the app and distribute it either in app stores, or internally within a company.

### Connecting a Canvasflow publication to Twixl Publisher

1. From 'Brand Manager' select the brand you wish to connect and click the `Settings` button.
2. Go to 'Publish Channel' and click the `+` button.
3. Select `Twixl Publisher` from the list of available channels.
4. Give your channel a name. (You may find it helpful to use the same name as your Twixl app.)
5. Enter your 'App' and 'Admin API' keys. You can find your Twixl Publisher API keys [here](https://platform.twixlmedia.com/account/api).
6. Select the publish option you wish to use (this can be changed at any time). Your options are;
 1. **Upload & Publish** (recommended) Publishing will upload articles to your Twixl Distribution account and make it available in your app. This is useful if you are working with Canvasflow generated content only and do not need to amend the content within Twixl Publisher before publishing.
 2. **Upload** Publishing from Canvasflow will upload content to your Twixl Distribution account but will **not** publish to your app. This setting is useful if you are working with additional Twixl content types and / or you want to edit your publication in Twixl before publishing.
7. Click `Add Channel` . Both the 'App' & 'Admin API' keys will be validated.
8. If the API validation is successful. the channel will be added to the current brand.  You now have the option to publish and manage articles within your Twixl Publisher distribution platform directly from Canvasflow.

### Notes

1. You must be logged into your Twixl Distribution Platform account and have the app you wish to connect to opened.
2. If the API key validation fails, you will be notified as to the cause.  If you see an error message, please ensure that your API keys are valid and entered correctly.
