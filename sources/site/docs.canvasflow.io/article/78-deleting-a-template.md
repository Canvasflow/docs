# Deleting a Template

As there are no dependencies on existing templates, they can be deleted from a publication at any time.

### To delete a template:

1. Navigate to the 'Template Manager' and locate the template you wish to edit.

From the `Options` column, click the `Trash` icon.  A confirmation popup will be displayed.

- ![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5a941af42c7d3a54cdfccc82/file-Zo3eysqD1n.png)

1. Click `Delete`.  The template will be deleted.

### Notes:

- Deleting a template is **permanent** and **cannot** be reversed.
- Deleting a template has **no** effect on articles already created from that template.
