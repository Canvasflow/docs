# Previewing content on Apple News

Canvasflow provides its own way to preview content on different devices, but it's always best practice to proof content on Apple News+.

### Previewing posts on Apple News Publisher:

1. Navigate to your [Apple News Publisher](https://www.icloud.com/newspublisher/)
2. Select _Articles_, then _Drafts_
3. Select _'Preview your articles'_
4. Select the link provided and send it to yourself to access on an iOS device

 [![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5e25a6912c7d3a7e9ae673c4/file-c28UnKsNSZ.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5e25a6912c7d3a7e9ae673c4/file-c28UnKsNSZ.png)

5. Alternatively if you are on a Mac you can open the link directly by clicking 'View' - this will open the article in the News app.

**Note**: You must be signed into the same Apple ID that is registered in your News Publisher account to be able to view draft content.
