# Cancel Account

We understand that Canvasflow isn't a perfect fit for everyone. 

In the event that you need to cancel/delete/close your account with us, please send a quick message to [support@canvasflow.io](mailto:ssupport@canvasflow.io?Subject=Cancel%20Account) and we'll be happy to take care of this for you.

### Note

- Deleting an account is **permanent and cannot be reversed**.
