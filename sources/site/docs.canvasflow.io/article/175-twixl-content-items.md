# Twixl Content Items

When a brand is connected to a Twixl App, the option of adding 'Twixl Content Items' becomes available from the Article Manager menu.

[![](https://d33v4339jhl8k0.cloudfront.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5afabaca042863158411c6c5/file-a0LZrQhgWU.png)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/571df1d49033600cce434499/images/5afabaca042863158411c6c5/file-a0LZrQhgWU.png)

Further information on 'Twixl Content items' can be found in the [Twixl support documentation](https://help.twixlmedia.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000718185-Working-with-content-items).
